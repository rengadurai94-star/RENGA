## 2. Voice guide (from the approved model scripts)

**Language:** Tanglish written in Roman script. Tamil carries emotion and questions ("Appo enna pannuveenga?"); English carries concepts and key terms ("feasibility", "growth corridor"). Roughly 50/50.

**What the model scripts do well (keep):**
- Opens on a money figure or a direct question: "Unga kitta ₹40 lakhs irukku…", "₹50 lakhs-ku vaangina property… today ₹1.5 crore".
- A quoted person as the hook: "Oruthavunga ketrikanga…", "Sir, good location. Good price. Vangikonga."
- Short stacked lines for lists, one idea per line, easy to cut to on-screen text.
- Reframe close: "Don't ask X. Ask Y."
- Measured, never hyped: "can appreciate", "may not", "realistically".
- Keyword CTA + "Nanri. Vanakkam."

**What the new direction adds:** more story, humour, surprise and retention devices than the model scripts currently have.

**Humour rules (premium-safe):**
- Humour comes from recognisable situations: the relative with free advice, the broker's "last plot sir", the mason's "adjust pannikkalam", WhatsApp-forward property tips, the uncle who "bought for ₹2 lakh in 1995".
- Laugh *with* the audience, never at a client, a community, a competitor or a named person.
- One or two light moments per reel, three or four per long-form. The expertise stays the point.
- No slapstick, no costumes, no trending audio memes that undercut the premium tone, unless the client approves a format.

**Over-used devices to rotate (already in 4+ model scripts):** "Don't ask X, ask Y" close; "indha reel-a enakku share pannunga"; "En goal enna?". Use each at most twice a month.

**Hard rules:**
- Never invent credentials, numbers, clients, projects or case studies. Use `[PLACEHOLDER: …]` and list it in the month's open questions.
- Infrastructure claims (AIIMS, TIDEL, SIPCOT, airport, ring road) say "announced / under construction / operational" as verified at the time of writing.
- Legal, tax, FEMA, REIT content is educational, with a disclaimer; no specific securities recommendations.
- Ezhil Enclave promotional content carries the TNRERA number if registered.
- Original identity: creative *principles* from finance-education creators are fine; their phrases, catchlines, formats and set styles are not.
