WHAT CHANGED AND WHY

Original version: "soil test panra process enna, bearing capacity number-a neenga
eppadi padikanum, cost comparison neenga eppadi pannanum" — again, a DIY-verification
tutorial. Same issue as Episode 1.

This version: the whole episode is built around two real projects side by side —
one where a soil test caught something, one where a neighbor skipped it. The viewer
isn't taught how to read a bearing-capacity number; they watch what happens when
nobody catches it, and what the presenter does differently. Closer keeps the
scarcity/personal-involvement CTA from Episode 1.

Same 5 points, same order, same series structure (recap, mid-CTA, closer teasing
Episode 3).

[POINT 1 — Every Plot Is Different]
Andha "next door plan follow pannalama" client-oda case-a continue pannuren.
Naan avangalukku clear-a sonnen — "adjacent plot-la work aana design, unga
plot-kum work aagum-nu guarantee illa."
Enthukku... andha area muழுவதுமே ஒரே soil illa.
Sila plots black cotton soil — rain-la swell aagும், summer-la shrink aagும்.
Sila plots filled land — kulama irundhadhu, mannu potu fill pannadhu.
Naan ovvoru project-layும், adjacent plots எவ்வளவு "similar" a therinjaalum,
independent soil test commission pannuven.
Idhu naan skip panra vishayam இல்ல — idhu naan every single time insist panra
vishayam.

[POINT 2 — What I Actually Verify]
Rendaavadhu — soil test-nu solli, oru junior engineer-a anupi report vaangittu
"done" nu solla mudியாது.
Naan personally, geotechnical engineer-oda site-ல் இருக்குven, bore holes எங்க
edukraanga-nu paarpen, report வந்தததும் naanே read panren, andha numbers-a
naanே interpret panren.
Andha "next door" house-ல் problem வந்ததுக்கு காரணம், அவங்க ஒரு quick, cheap
soil test வெச்சு, actual bearing depth verify pannாமலே foundation design
finalize pannாங்க.
Idhu quality control-oda oru layer — client idha kavanikkவே vேண்டாம், idhu
naan handle panra vishayam.

[POINT 3 — Bearing Capacity Decides the Foundation Type]
Moonaavadhu case — vera oru project, Coimbatore side.
Report vandhadhu, bearing capacity romba குறைவா irundhuchu.
Oru normal isolated footing போதாது-nu clear-a therinjuchu.
Naan client-kitta "bearing capacity enna, calculation enna"-nu explain panni
neram waste pannாம, straight-a raft foundation-ku switch pannivechen,
structural engineer-oda calculation-a naanே verify pannivechen.
Client-ku theriyாதே, ஆனா அந்த decision அந்த வீட்டோட next 50 years-a
protect pannuchu.

[POINT 4 — Water Table]
Naalaavadhu — ECR side ஒரு project, water table romba mேல-ஆ irundhuchu.
Naan excavation ஆரம்பிக்குறதுக்கு முன்னாடியே இது கண்டுபிடிச்சேன், waterproofing-a

Idhu naan catch pannாம போயிருந்தா, excavation-oda naடுவுல தண்ணீர் ஊறி,

Naan indha check-a project-oda Day 1-லேயே build panniduven — client idha
கேட்கவே தேவை இல்ல.

[POINT 5 — Why I Never Compromise on This]
ஐந்தாவது — soil test cost, ஒரு multi-crore project-oda budget-ல ஒரு rounding
error தான்.
Neriya contractors, "time waste, skip panniduvom" nu client-a convince
pannிடுவாங்க.
Naan ஒரு project-லும் இதை skip pannமாட்டேன் — client rush pannினாலும் கூட.
ஆரம்பத்துல சொன்ன "next door" family, இந்த ஒரு decision-ஐ skip பண்ணதால,
lakhs-ல repair cost எடுத்தாங்க. Ennoda client-க்கு அந்த problem-வே வரலை.
Idhu thaan ஒரு premium build-a, ஒரு average build-a irundhu vेறுபடுத்துற
விஷயம்.
