# "What If the Sun Disappeared for 24 Hours?"
## Full 8-Minute Production Package — 1080p / 16:9

**Format:** Faceless curiosity-science explainer (same build as `two-moons-production-package.md`)
**Total runtime:** ~7:50
**Total shots:** 46 (10s each, trim as marked)
**VO:** Warm, curious, slightly playful narrator, conversational, ~155 wpm (~1,220 words)
**Why this one next:** the two-moons video's end card already promises it — this is the payoff to a hook the channel has already planted.

---

## Why this topic works

- **Instantly visual** — darkness, frost, frozen oceans. Every beat has an obvious shot.
- **Delayed-reveal hook** — nothing happens for 8 minutes and 20 seconds. That gap *is* the hook.
- **Two-stage payoff** — "24 hours" is the promise in the title, but the back half escalates to "what if it never came back," which is where the retention lives.
- **Hopeful kicker** — life on Earth doesn't fully end, and that twist earns the comment section.

## ASSEMBLY GUIDE

### Tools
- **Editor:** CapCut or Premiere Pro
- **VO:** ElevenLabs, or Higgsfield `generate_audio`
- **Music:** "cinematic curious documentary", 80–95 BPM. Drop the music out entirely at Shot 10 (the blackout) for two seconds — silence sells that cut harder than any sting.
- **SFX:** deep space drones, ice cracking, wind that stops abruptly at Shot 37

### Timeline
1. Clips on V1 in order; VO on A1; music bed at −22 LUFS on A2; SFX on A3.
2. 0.2s crossfades throughout — **except** Shot 9→10, which must be a hard cut on the frame.
3. Colour grade in two halves: warm golden for Shots 1–9, then progressively colder and bluer from Shot 10 onward. Shots 42–46 nearly monochrome, until the final sunrise returns full warmth.
4. Lower-third text for the big numbers: **"8 min 20 sec"**, **"67,000 mph"**, **"−100°F in one year"**, **"40,000 years to the nearest star"**.
5. Light film grain throughout; add subtle letterbox during Shots 33–37 for the quiet stretch.
6. Chapter markers in the description at each section boundary.
