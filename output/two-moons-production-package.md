## ASSEMBLY GUIDE

### Tools
- **Editor:** CapCut (free, faceless-friendly) or Adobe Premiere Pro
- **Voice-over:** ElevenLabs (recommended voices: "Brian", "Charlotte", "Adam"), or Higgsfield `generate_audio`
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound or Artlist — search "cinematic curious documentary", BPM 80–95
- **SFX:** Pixabay or Freesound — earthquake rumbles, water surges, deep space drones

### Timeline
1. Drop all 47 clips on V1 in order.
2. Place voice-over on A1, music bed at -22 LUFS on A2, SFX on A3.
3. Add 0.2s crossfade between every clip.
4. Add light film grain + subtle 4:3 letterbox masks during dramatic beats for cinematic feel.
5. Color grade: lift blacks slightly, push shadows blue, highlights warm orange (Bright Side palette).
6. Add lower-third text overlays for big numbers: **"40 ft tides"**, **"24 → 40 hour days"**, **"100 years"**.
7. Add chapter markers in YouTube description at each beat.

### Thumbnail suggestion
- Earth with two glowing moons in sky
- Big yellow text: **"2 MOONS?!"**
- Shocked face cutout in corner
- Red arrow pointing at second moon

### YouTube SEO

- **Tags:** what if, two moons, space, bright side style, second moon, earth, astronomy, science explainer
- **Description:** First two lines should restate the hook. Include timestamps.
