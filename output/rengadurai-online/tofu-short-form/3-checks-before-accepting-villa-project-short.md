# Short-Form (Reels / YouTube Shorts) — TOFU
## "3 Things I Check Before Accepting a Premium Villa Project"

**Stage:** TOFU (Awareness) — per `../00-strategy/funnel-mapping.md`
**Source hook:** brief §10, listed verbatim as a short-form hook example
**Format:** Vertical 9:16, Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / Facebook repost
**Runtime:** 45-55 seconds
**Language:** Tanglish, punchy delivery — faster pace than the long-form
series, single idea, no case setup

---

## Why this is TOFU, not MOFU (per the new workflow)

Picked from the funnel-mapping doc's priority list — this is the biggest
open gap (no TOFU content exists at all), and this specific hook needed
no real project facts or founder input to script, unlike the two blocked
category gaps.

**Applying the stage checklist before writing:**
1. **Audience:** cold/scrolling, doesn't know Rengadurai yet.
2. **Depth:** shallow by design — three short beats, no explanation of
   *why* each check matters beyond one line. That depth is what the
   long-form VKP series and case studies are for.
3. **CTA:** soft. No "send your project details" here — that's a BOFU
   mismatch for a viewer who's three seconds into discovering him. Ask is
   just: follow.
4. **Length:** under a minute. Resisted the urge to expand any of the 3
   points into a mini-explanation — that would turn this into a MOFU
   script wearing a TOFU hook, exactly what the funnel doc warns against.

## VISUAL DIRECTION

| Beat | Visual |
|---|---|
| Hook | Presenter direct-to-camera, close crop, confident half-smile — the "I turn people down" line needs to land with authority, not arrogance |
| Point 1 | Quick cut: document/title deed close-up |
| Point 2 | Quick cut: presenter sketching/gesturing a floor plan concept |
| Point 3 | Quick cut: presenter shaking head at a lower-quality material sample |
| Closer | Back to presenter, direct address, confident nod |

**Captions:** burned-in captions for the full spoken line, not just the
on-screen text cues above — most Reels/Shorts viewers watch muted.

## CTA

**Soft, per TOFU stage rules:** "Follow பண்ணுங்க" only. No project-enquiry
CTA, no brand name mention (Shape Rock/Urban Mark/Square Mount) — those
are BOFU-stage asks and would mismatch a viewer three seconds into
discovering him.
