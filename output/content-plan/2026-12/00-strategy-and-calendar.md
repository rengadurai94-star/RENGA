## 1. Why this month looks the way it does

**December is homecoming month.** Many NRIs travel home for school holidays, weddings, temple visits and Margazhi. For a few weeks the NRI audience is physically in Tamil Nadu, meeting relatives, seeing plots, signing documents and getting advice from everyone. That makes December the best month of the year for NRI content.

**December is also the end of the north-east monsoon** in Tamil Nadu. Rain shows up drainage and water-logging problems on a plot, which gives us a strong, seasonal site-inspection angle.

**Year-end is the moment for "health checks" and "what's coming next year".** People take stock of their money and assets, and infrastructure timelines are back in the news. AIIMS Madurai, TIDEL Park Madurai and Melur SIPCOT all have 2026–27 dates attached.

**Month theme: _"Before you sign anything."_** Every piece helps someone make a cleaner decision on documents, costs, design or timing. It builds on the positioning ("Think before you build") without repeating any Month 0 topic.

### Business objectives this month
1. **NRI leads.** Five pieces speak directly to NRIs, timed to the first half of December when they're in India and can meet.
2. **Authority on documents and due diligence.** No Month 0 script covered land documents; this is a gap where competitors stay shallow.
3. **Homebuilder trust.** Cost, design-for-climate and design-for-the-future pieces show the architect's thinking.
4. **Ezhil Enclave.** One case-study reel, placed mid-month when visiting NRIs and locals can come to the site. Only 1 of the 19 pieces is promotional.

## 2. New brand devices (original to Rengadurai)

| Device | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **"PLOT TWIST" card** | A full-screen card (brand colour, slam sound) introducing a surprising fact mid-video. A real-estate pun that becomes his signature pattern interrupt. | Every long-form (2–3×), 6 reels |
| **Three recurring characters**, all played by Rengadurai with a quick costume prop and on-screen name tag (no extra cast needed) | **Broker Bala** (towel on shoulder, phone always ringing: "Last plot sir!") · **WhatsApp Mama** (reading glasses, forwards everything) · **Adjust Anna** (helmet, "adjust pannikkalam sir", continuing the Month 0 reel) | LF3, LF4, R10, R13, R15 |
| **"Site-la oru nimisham"** | A one-minute aside filmed on a real site, where he points at something specific | LF1, LF3 |

The characters are affectionate, not mocking. Brokers, relatives and contractors are part of the audience's life and some will be watching. The joke is always the *habit*, never the person or a community.

> If Rengadurai isn't comfortable playing characters (intake Q17 is still open), each character line works as a quoted voice: "Broker solluvaaru…" with the name tag on screen. The scripts still work.
