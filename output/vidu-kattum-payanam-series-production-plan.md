# "வீடு கட்டும் பயணம்" — Production & Playlist Plan
## How to shoot, edit, and publish all 8 episodes in order

---

## The one constraint to plan around

Unlike the faceless AI-generated projects in this repo (`runway-execution-plan.md`,
`two-moons-production-package.md`), this series is **you, on camera, at real sites**.
That means two very different production tracks:

1. **Presenter (talking-head) segments** — hook, setup, recap, mid-episode, closer —
   don't depend on any specific construction stage. You can shoot all 8 episodes'
   presenter pieces in **1-2 days**, back-to-back, against one consistent backdrop.
2. **B-roll / site footage** — foundation pour, RCC structure, brickwork, wiring,
   flooring, kitchen install — can only be filmed when a real project is actually
   at that stage. You almost certainly don't have one site hitting all 8 stages in
   sequence on your filming schedule.

**Recommendation:** don't wait for one project to go through all 8 stages start to
finish (that's 8-12 months before Episode 1 even publishes). Instead, source B-roll
from **whichever active client site is currently at the matching stage** — you likely
have 3-4 projects running in parallel at different stages right now. Shoot the
presenter track this week, and slot in B-roll opportunistically as each site reaches
the relevant stage. Publish Episode 1 as soon as its B-roll is ready; you don't need
all 8 in hand before starting the upload cadence.

## SHOOT ORDER

### Phase 1 — Presenter track (batch, 1-2 days)
Shoot all 8 episodes' to-camera segments in one sitting, in this order (matches
script numbering, same backdrop/framing/mic setup for every episode so they cut
together consistently):

| Order | Episode | Segments to shoot | Est. time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ep 1 | Hook, series intro, 4 points, mid-episode, recap, closer | 25 min |
| 2 | Ep 2 | Hook, recap, 4 points, mid-episode, recap, closer | 25 min |
| 3 | Ep 3 | Hook, recap, 4 points, mid-episode, recap, closer | 30 min |
| 4 | Ep 4 | Hook, recap, 4 points, mid-episode, recap, closer | 25 min |
| 5 | Ep 5 | Hook, recap, 4 points, mid-episode, recap, closer | 30 min |
| 6 | Ep 6 | Hook, recap, 4 points, mid-episode, recap, closer | 25 min |
| 7 | Ep 7 | Hook, recap, 4 points + wardrobe note, mid-episode, recap, closer | 35 min |
| 8 | Ep 8 | Hook, recap, 4 points, mid-episode, series wrap-up/closer | 25 min |

**Tips:**
- Shoot 2-3 takes of every recap/closer line — these get reused as pattern-cues,
  easy to pick the cleanest later.
- Record the Ep 8 series wrap-up last — say it after you've just said all 7 other
  intros, so the "we covered every stage" framing feels genuine, not scripted.
- Wear the same outfit / use the same framing across all 8 — viewers binge these
  back to back, and a visual mismatch breaks the "series" feel.

### Phase 2 — B-roll (opportunistic, matched to real site progress)

| Episode | B-roll needed | Where to source it |
|---|---|---|
| Ep 1 | Plan/drawing table shots, plot photos | Any current client's plan-approval stage |
| Ep 2 | Excavation, soil test in progress | Any site currently at excavation |
| Ep 3 | Rebar cage, concrete pour, curing | Any site currently pouring RCC |
| Ep 4 | Brickwork, plumb-bob check, plastering | Any site at brick/plaster stage |
| Ep 5 | Open walls with conduit/pipe runs | Any site before wall-closing |
| Ep 6 | Tile-laying, painting in progress | Any site at finishing stage |
| Ep 7 | Modular kitchen install, wardrobe fitting | Any site at interior-fit stage |
| Ep 8 | Completed home, water test, handover | Any recently handed-over project |

If a matching stage isn't available in time for an episode's scheduled upload, two
fallbacks: (a) use footage from a already-completed past project (many of these
shots — cracked wall, rusted steel, cover blocks — don't need to be from an ongoing
job), or (b) push that episode's upload date rather than publish without real B-roll
— stock/generic construction footage will visibly clash with your authentic,
expertise-led tone.

## POST-PRODUCTION CHECKLIST (per episode)

1. Cut presenter track as the spine; drop in B-roll at the cutaway points marked
   in each script's "Visual Direction" table.
2. Burn in the recurring **series title card** at 0:00 of the "series recap" beat
   (same animation/music sting every episode — this is what makes it read as a
   series, not 8 unrelated uploads).
3. Add the fixed **"EP X"** badge to the corner of every thumbnail — same position,
   same font, every episode.
4. Add lower-third captions for key numbers/terms (bearing capacity, Fe500, BWP ply,
   etc.) — this audience skims for exactly these facts.
5. End-screen: link to the **next episode specifically**, not a generic subscribe
   card. Episode 8 ends by linking back to the full playlist instead.

## YOUTUBE PLAYLIST SETUP

1. Create the playlist **before** uploading Episode 1: "வீடு கட்டும் பயணம் — Foundation to Move-in".
2. Playlist description: one line stating the 8-stage journey + a note that new
   episodes drop weekly (or your actual cadence).
3. Upload episodes in strict numeric order — YouTube playlists default to
   upload order unless manually re-sorted, so this avoids Episode 5 appearing
   before Episode 3.
4. Pin a comment on **every** episode linking to the playlist, not just Episode 1 —
   viewers land on individual episodes via search/suggested far more than via the
   playlist page.
5. Suggested cadence: **1 episode/week**. Weekly is frequent enough to keep binge
   momentum but gives you room for the B-roll-matching constraint above. If two
   sites hit their stage in the same week, you can bank an episode ahead.
