**Updated:** 28 July 2026

## 1. The Core Instruction

Do not build Rengadurai Online as a generic construction tips page.

Build it as a premium architecture and real estate intelligence brand for
high-value homebuilders, NRIs, landowners, and serious real estate
investors in Tamil Nadu.

The content must make the audience feel:

> "Rengadurai is the person I should speak to before I build, invest,
> develop land, or trust a developer with my property."

## 2. Who Rengadurai Is

Architect, real estate developer, and development advisor based in Tamil
Nadu.

**Professional background:**
- B.Arch, Hindustan University, passed out in 2017
- M.Arch completed in 2021
- ~10 years in architecture, construction, interiors, real estate
  development, and development advisory
- Founder of Shape Rock Design & Build
- Founder of Urban Mark Interior
- Partner in AVS Lifescape LLP
- Founder of Square Mount Development Partners

**Positioning:** Architect. Real estate developer. Property decision
advisor. **Not** a contractor, civil engineer, site engineer, or ordinary
construction company owner.

> Rengadurai helps people make better property decisions before they risk
> big money.

One main content engine — Rengadurai Online across Instagram, YouTube,
Facebook, LinkedIn, X. Do not split creator energy into multiple
influencer pages. Supporting business pages are portfolio/proof pages,
not daily content channels.

## 6. Target Audience

**Primary:**
- HNI clients planning homes/villas above ₹1 crore
- NRIs planning to build, invest, or develop property in Tamil Nadu
- Landowners in Chennai, Madurai, and other prime TN locations
- Investors planning ₹1 crore+ real estate investments
- New/existing developers needing project structuring
- Existing landowners wanting to convert land into a revenue asset
- Interior clients with budgets above ₹10-15 lakhs

**Secondary (does not control content direction — grows reach only):**
- General homeowners, first-time builders, apartment buyers, people
  learning construction/real estate basics

## 7. Past Mistakes To Correct

1. **Too much low-ticket construction content** — basic budgets, switch
   boxes, steel, generic tips attract ₹40-50L leads, not the ideal
   architect-led premium client.
2. **Positioning sounded like contractor/content educator** — should
   sound like "I will help you make the right property decision before
   you risk crores," not "I will build your house cheaply."
3. **Weak lead qualification** — "comment price" CTAs attract everyone;
   new CTAs should attract serious project/investment enquiries.
4. **Not enough premium proof** — need actual project scale, value,
   location, process, design thinking, trust systems. Reels alone aren't
   enough; long-form case studies are needed.
5. **No clear platform role** — IG/YouTube/FB/LinkedIn/X shouldn't carry
   identical content; angle and format must fit each platform.
6. **Too much "budget" language** — avoid "low budget," "cheap," "40 lakh
   house," "simple house construction." Replace with "premium planning,"
   "costly mistakes," "investment risk," "long-term value," "quality
   control," "asset protection."

## 8. Content Topics (7 Categories)

### Category 1: Premium Architecture And Villas
Purpose: attract ₹1cr+ home/villa clients. Use real projects as proof:
- 7000 sqft sea-facing villa, Palavakkam
- Two villas with pools: ~₹1cr and ~₹1.5cr
- ₹3cr villa, Nolambur
- ₹5cr individual house, Mandaveli
- ₹2cr individual house, Parithipattu

Angles: what makes a villa premium beyond elevation; why expensive homes
still fail without good planning; space planning mistakes in luxury
homes; privacy/wind/heat/maintenance/long-term value; how an architect
thinks before construction begins.

### Category 2: Project Case Studies
Purpose: convert proof into trust. Every project video follows:
1. Project identity (location, type, size/value range)
2. Client context (what the client wanted)
3. Site/design challenge
4. Rengadurai's decision-making
5. Construction/interior/real estate lesson
6. What similar clients should learn
7. CTA for qualified enquiries

Example titles: "Inside a 7000 Sqft Sea-Facing Villa in Palavakkam,"
"How We Planned a 5 Crore Individual House in Mandaveli," "1.5 Crore
Villa With Swimming Pool: What Makes It Premium?," "2.5 Crore Service
Apartment Project in Velachery," "What I Check Before Accepting a Premium
Villa Project."

### Category 3: NRI Homebuilding And Remote Trust
Purpose: attract NRIs building/investing in TN without being cheated.
Topics: remote construction monitoring; documents to verify before
buying land/apartments; choosing an architect/developer from abroad; why
cheap execution becomes expensive; monthly reporting/vendor
control/quality control; how NRIs lose money and how to avoid it.
**CTA:** "For NRI construction or real estate advisory, DM CONSULT."

### Category 4: Landowner And JV Education
Purpose: attract landowners with valuable property in Chennai, Madurai,
etc. Topics: sell/hold/lease/develop/JV decision; how landowners get
trapped by bad developers; what to verify before signing a development
agreement; converting ancestral property into revenue; apartment vs
plotted development; why legal cases take years and prevention is
better; how Square Mount Development Partners helps structure projects
safely.
**CTA:** "For land development or JV advisory, contact Square Mount
Development Partners."
**Important:** No public allegations against builders/projects unless
facts are verified from public documents, RERA/legal records, or
credible news sources.

### Category 5: Real Estate Investment Intelligence
Purpose: attract ₹1cr+ investors; build future REIT/investment authority.
Topics: apartment vs land vs plotted development; rental yield vs
appreciation; liquidity/resale risk; title/approval/loanability/
litigation risk; what makes a real estate asset safe; REITs in India;
how serious investors should evaluate real estate.
**Tone:** educational, careful, evidence-based. **Do not promise
returns.**

### Category 6: Premium Interiors
Purpose: attract ₹10-15L+ interior leads; support Urban Mark Interior.
Topics: why expensive interiors can look cheap; space planning before
material selection; lighting mistakes; storage/circulation/maintenance/
resale value; apartment/villa/commercial case studies; design concept
renders clearly labelled as renders.
**CTA:** "For premium interiors above Rs. 15 lakhs, enquire through
Urban Mark Interior."

### Category 7: Founder Journey And Authority
Purpose: make Rengadurai human, trusted, memorable. Topics: why he
separated personal/architecture/interior/advisory brands; lessons from
10 years across architecture, construction, interiors, real estate;
lessons from acre-scale land development; what he learned from clients,
developers, landowners, investors; why he wants to build a real estate
investment ecosystem in the next 5 years.

## 9. Long-Form YouTube Strategy

Trust-building engine. Frequency: minimum 4 long-form/month, better 1/
week. Length: 8-20 min for most, 20-35 min for deep case studies/
landowner/investor topics.

Formats: premium project walkthroughs, architecture case studies, NRI
guidance, landowner/JV advisory, real estate investment breakdowns,
founder/developer journey.

Every long-form video must answer: Why should a premium client trust
Rengadurai? What does he know that an ordinary contractor/broker doesn't?
What risk does he help the client avoid? What decision does he help the
viewer make?

## 10. Short-Form Strategy

Push toward long-form YouTube, project enquiries, NRI/landowner/investor
advisory. Frequency: IG 3-5 Reels/week, YouTube Shorts 4-5/week
(repurposed), Facebook reposts the strongest Reels.

**Hook examples:** "A 1 crore home can still fail if planning is wrong."
/ "Never give your land for JV before checking this." / "This is why
NRIs lose money in construction." / "3 things I check before accepting a
premium villa project." / "Why cheap contractors become expensive." /
"This is what makes a villa feel premium." / "Before buying a 1 crore
apartment, check this first."

**Avoid:** "build cheapest," "low budget house tips," "comment price" as
main CTA, generic construction hacks that attract low-ticket leads.

## 11. Platform Roles

- **Instagram** — main discovery/trust platform. Reels, short case-study
  clips, carousels, Stories, project updates, polls/Q&A, DM lead gen.
  Strong hooks, Tamil/Tanglish-friendly, premium but simple.
- **YouTube** — deep authority/conversion platform. Long-form case
  studies, walkthroughs, landowner/NRI/investor education. Shorts up to
  3 min for eligible uploads, but 30-90 sec for most.
- **Facebook** — local trust, family decision-maker reach, retargeting,
  older audience. Keep personal profile (Professional Mode) if active;
  also create a Rengadurai Online Page for ads/team/business identity.
  Don't blindly convert everything.
- **LinkedIn** — HNI/NRI/investor/developer/landowner authority. Founder's
  personal profile is the main channel (Pages for Shape Rock/Urban
  Mark/Square Mount can come later). More professional, less Reel-like.
- **X** — sharp opinions, real estate commentary, credibility, idea
  testing. Short opinions, threads, project lessons, risk observations,
  TN property trend commentary.

## 12. CTA Framework

| Audience | CTA |
|---|---|
| Premium homes | "For premium home and villa projects above Rs. 1 crore, send your project details." |
| NRI | "For NRI homebuilding or real estate advisory in Tamil Nadu, DM CONSULT." |
| Landowner | "For land development or JV advisory, contact Square Mount Development Partners." |
| Investor | "For Rs. 1 crore+ real estate investment advisory, send your requirement." |
| Interiors | "For premium interiors above Rs. 15 lakhs, enquire through Urban Mark Interior." |

Do not use only generic CTAs like "DM for price" — attracts low-quality
enquiries.

## 13. Content Packaging Rules

1. Every premium project → 1 YouTube long-form + 3-5 Reels/Shorts + 1
   carousel + 5-10 Stories + 1 LinkedIn case study post + 1 X thread.
2. Same asset, different platform angle: IG = hook/visual impact, YouTube
   = depth/trust, Facebook = local trust/shareability, LinkedIn =
   professional authority, X = sharp insight/opinion.
3. Do not copy-paste the same caption everywhere.
4. Label renders clearly: Completed project / Ongoing project / Proposed
   design / Render walkthrough / Concept design.
5. Never overclaim.

## 14. Tone Of Voice

**Be:** authoritative but simple, practical, premium but not arrogant,
Tamil Nadu-rooted, protective of clients, architect-first, real estate
intelligent, honest about risk, confident without hype.

**Avoid:** cheap viral language, fake luxury words, exaggerated promises,
unverified legal allegations, sounding like a broker/contractor, too
much budget-house content, too much "comment price" content.

## 15. Monthly Content Mix

- 6 premium architecture/project posts
- 4 NRI trust/safety posts
- 4 landowner/JV posts
- 4 real estate investment posts
- 3 interiors posts
- 3 founder journey/proof posts
- 2 general construction education posts — **framed for premium
  decision-making**

## 16. Success Metrics

Not just views/followers. Track: ₹1cr+ home/villa enquiries, ₹15L+
interior enquiries, NRI enquiries, landowner/JV advisory enquiries,
investor conversations, YouTube watch time, profile visits from premium
content, saves/shares on advisory content, qualified DM conversations,
consultation bookings. **Real success = better-quality leads, not more
followers.**

## 17. The Final Rule For The Content Team

Before writing any script, caption, or post, ask:

> "Will this make Rengadurai look like a premium architect, real estate
> developer, and trusted property decision advisor?"

If yes, create it. If it only makes him look like a generic construction
tips creator, rewrite it.
