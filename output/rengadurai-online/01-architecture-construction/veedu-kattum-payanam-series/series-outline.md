## Recurring series elements

- **Series recap line (every episode):** references the previous episode's
  stage before introducing the current one.
- **Voice:** case-first hook ("Last month, a client...") → principle
  statement after every case → composed recap → CTA.
- **Thumbnail template:** presenter reviewing real project material,
  composed/authoritative expression, elegant text, no warning icons, no
  checklist ticks, "EP N" badge in a fixed corner position across all
  episodes.
- **End-screen:** links to the next episode specifically (Episode 8 links
  to the full playlist instead, since it's the finale).

## What changed in this restructure

1. Moved from `output/vidu-kattum-payanam-*` (flat, mixed with unrelated
   projects) into `output/rengadurai-online/01-architecture-construction/`
   to match the brief's category structure.
2. Original mass-market/DIY-checklist versions of all 8 episodes were
   archived to `../../legacy-archive/veedu-kattum-payanam-originals/` —
   kept for reference only, not for use per brief §7.
3. Every episode's closer CTA was rewritten to match brief §12's exact
   CTA framework instead of a generic "connect via description" line.
   Episode 7 now routes to Urban Mark Interior specifically since its
   content is interiors, not architecture/construction.
4. Production plan (`production-plan.md`, moved from the old flat file)
   still applies as-is — shoot order and B-roll sourcing guidance are
   unaffected by the reframe.
5. **Positioning correction (all 8 episodes):** the brief is explicit that
   Rengadurai should not read as "a contractor, civil engineer, site
   engineer, or ordinary construction company owner" — only as "Architect.
   Real estate developer. Property decision advisor." Several episodes
   originally had him personally performing site-engineer/QC-technician
   tasks (checking cement manufacturing dates, physically testing steel,
   holding a plumb bob, testing earthing with a multimeter, cutting
   plywood samples by hand). These were reworked so he mandates the
   quality-certification/testing protocol and personally reviews the
   resulting reports/certificates to make the accept/reject call — his
   site engineers and consultants perform the physical testing. Each
   affected episode's VOICE NOTE documents exactly what changed.
