# Rengadurai — Personal Brand Execution Playbook

The strategy says **what** to build: an architect-led advisory brand for high-value property decisions in Tamil Nadu. This playbook covers **how**: positioning lines, profile rewrites, the lead funnel, posting cadence, a 30-day launch calendar and ready-to-shoot scripts.

> **Name check before anything goes live:** the brief uses both "Rangadurai" and "Rengadurai". Pick one spelling and use it on every handle, bio, thumbnail, lower-third and document. This playbook uses **Rengadurai**.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-line positioning**
> Architect. Developer. Advisor. I help families, NRIs and landowners in Tamil Nadu make ₹1 Cr+ property decisions they won't regret.

**Category we own:** *Think before you build.* Competitors sell design (architects), execution (interior/turnkey firms), site tracking (NRI build platforms) or paperwork (consultants). Rengadurai sells **the right decision**, then delivers it.

**Proof stack** (use in this order, everywhere):
1. 100+ homes designed
2. 10+ years across architecture, construction and development
3. Real ₹1–5 Cr projects shown on camera, with numbers and lessons
4. Client voices (NRIs and landowners first)

**Messaging rules**
| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Before you spend ₹1 Cr, here's what to check" | "Luxury", "world-class", "dream home" |
| "What this project taught us" | "Best architect in Chennai" |
| "Here's the trade-off, here's what I'd pick" | "Cheapest", "lowest cost", "hassle-free" |
| Specific numbers, sq.ft, timelines, rupees | Vague promises |
| "A common mistake I see" | Naming or blaming a competitor or a specific builder |

**Price framing (counters the commoditisation threat):** never compete on per-sq.ft rate. Frame the fee against the cost of a wrong decision: *"A ₹2 Cr home with a bad plot orientation or a weak JV agreement loses more than any design fee."*

## 3. Lead generation system (fixes "weak CTAs")

### 3.1 Lead magnets — one per audience
| Audience | Lead magnet | CTA keyword |
|---|---|---|
| HNI homebuilder | "Before You Spend ₹1 Crore: 21-Point Pre-Build Checklist" (PDF) | **PLAN** |
| NRI | "Building in Tamil Nadu from Abroad: The NRI Control Kit" (documents list, payment-milestone template, site-update format) | **NRI** |
| Landowner | "Sell or Develop? Landowner Decision Guide + JV Agreement Red Flags" | **LAND** |
| Investor | "Land vs Villa vs Apartment: ₹1 Cr Comparison Sheet" | **INVEST** |
| Interior client (₹10–15 L+) | "Interior Budget Planner: Where to Spend, Where to Save" | **INTERIOR** |

### 3.2 Funnel
```
Content (reel / video / post)
  → CTA: "Comment PLAN" or "DM NRI"
    → Auto-DM (ManyChat or Meta inbox automation) sends the PDF + 2 qualifying questions
      → Landing page form (name, city/country, project type, budget band, timeline)
        → Budget ≥ ₹1 Cr or interior ≥ ₹10 L → calendar link for a 20-min discovery call
        → Below threshold → email/WhatsApp nurture list (secondary audience, still valuable for reach)
```

**Qualifying questions in the auto-DM:**
1. What are you planning — new home, interiors, land development, or investment?
2. Rough budget: under ₹50 L / ₹50 L–1 Cr / ₹1–3 Cr / ₹3 Cr+?

### 3.3 CTA bank — rotate, one CTA per post
- "Comment **PLAN** and I'll send you the 21-point checklist."
- "Building from abroad? DM **NRI** for the control kit I give my overseas clients."
- "Own land in Chennai or Madurai? DM **LAND** before you sign anything."
- "Save this before your first meeting with an architect."
- "Send this to the person in your family handling the site."
- "Planning a ₹1 Cr+ build in the next 12 months? Link in bio for a 20-min call."

### 3.4 Lead tracking
One shared sheet or CRM: date, source platform, post, keyword, segment, budget band, status (new → qualified → call booked → proposal → won). Review weekly: **which posts produce qualified calls**, not just which get views.

### Batch-shoot day (once every 2 weeks, ~6 hours)
- 2 long-form videos (1 studio explainer, 1 site walkthrough)
- 8–10 talking-head reel scripts
- 15 min of B-roll per active site (drone, details, team at work, material close-ups)
- 2 founder photos sets for LinkedIn/X

### Repurposing chain
```
1 YouTube long-form
 → 3 Shorts / Reels (best 30–45s moments)
 → 1 Instagram carousel + 1 LinkedIn document (key points)
 → 1 X thread (the argument in 6–8 posts)
 → 1 LinkedIn text post (the founder's opinion)
 → Facebook: full video + reel
```

## 6. Ready-to-shoot scripts

### Reel 1 — "Who I help" (pin) — 45s
- **Hook (0–3s):** "If you're about to spend a crore on property in Tamil Nadu, watch this first."
- **Body:** "I'm Rengadurai. In 10 years I've designed over 100 homes — and I've also been on the developer side. So I see your project from both ends: how it looks, and how the money works. I help three kinds of people: families building ₹1 Cr+ homes, NRIs building from abroad, and landowners deciding whether to sell or develop."
- **Proof cut:** 3 quick project shots.
- **CTA:** "If that's you, comment PLAN and I'll send you the checklist I use before every project."

### Reel 2 — "Architect vs developer" — 40s
- **Hook:** "Same plot. Two professionals. Completely different answers."
- **Body:** Split screen. *Architect sees:* light, orientation, how the family will live. *Developer sees:* FSI, approvals, resale, cost per sq.ft. "Most people hire one of them. The best decisions come from both."
- **CTA:** "Save this for your first site visit."

### Reel 3 — "3 mistakes NRIs make" — 50s
- **Hook:** "Building a home in Tamil Nadu from Dubai, Singapore or the US? Don't do these three things."
- **Body:** (1) Releasing payments by time instead of by verified milestones. (2) Letting a relative 'manage the site' without a written scope. (3) Finalising design over WhatsApp photos instead of drawings and a material schedule.
- **CTA:** "DM NRI and I'll send the control kit I give my overseas clients."

### Reel 4 — "Sell or develop?" — 50s
- **Hook:** "You own land in Chennai. A developer offers you a joint venture. Before you sign — three questions."
- **Body:** (1) What's your share — in built-up area or in money, and when exactly do you get it? (2) Who carries approval and delay risk, and what's the penalty? (3) What happens if the developer stops midway?
- **CTA:** "DM LAND for the red-flag list. And always have the agreement reviewed by your own lawyer."

### Reel 5 — Myth vs fact — 30s
- **Hook:** "Myth: a bigger house means a better investment."
- **Body:** "Fact: unused space costs you to build, furnish, clean and maintain — for decades. A well-planned 3,200 sq.ft home often lives better than a badly planned 4,500."
- **CTA:** "Send this to someone finalising their floor plan."

### YouTube long-form outline — "Architect vs Builder vs Developer"
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:45):** a real (anonymised) story where the wrong order of decisions cost a client money.
2. **Who does what (0:45–4:00):** architect, builder/contractor, developer, consultant — on-screen table.
3. **Where things go wrong (4:00–9:00):** gaps between roles — approvals, cost overruns, design changes mid-build.
4. **The integrated approach (9:00–13:00):** one project shown from land to handover.
5. **How to choose (13:00–16:00):** 5 questions to ask any professional before you hire them.
6. **CTA (16:00–end):** checklist via link in description; "comment your city and plot size, I'll answer the best questions in the next video."

**Title options:** "Architect vs Builder vs Developer — Who Protects Your ₹1 Crore?" / "Don't Hire Anyone to Build Your Home Until You Watch This"
**Thumbnail:** Rengadurai's face + three labelled hard-hats + "WHO DO YOU NEED?"

## 8. Guardrails

- **Legal, tax and FEMA content:** keep it educational, say "verify with your lawyer/CA", and bring a qualified professional on camera for detailed rules. Rules change — date-stamp anything regulation-specific.
- **No competitor call-outs.** Show the better way; never name or shame a firm, builder or individual (the brief's "doesn't make unverified allegations").
- **Client consent** in writing before showing a home, a face, a budget or an address. Blur house numbers and street signs.
- **Numbers must be real.** Use budget bands if exact figures are confidential, but never round up.
- **"Architect reacts" content** uses generic, anonymised or openly published plans only.
