# Rengadurai Online — Funnel Mapping (Awareness / Consideration / Conversion)

Companion to `content-strategy-brief.md` and `content-tracker.md`. The brief
organizes content by **topic category** (7 categories). This doc organizes
the same content by **funnel stage** — a different cut of the same library,
so we can see whether we're actually balanced across the buyer journey, not
just across topics.

**Rule going forward:** every new script gets a funnel stage decided
*before* writing, not after. The stage determines length, depth, hook
style, and CTA intensity — deciding it upfront prevents writing a 9-minute
deep-dive when the goal was a 60-second cold-audience hook, or a soft
subscribe-CTA when the goal was a direct enquiry push.

---

## The three stages, defined for this brand

### TOFU — Awareness
**Who it's for:** people who don't know Rengadurai exists yet. Cold or
lightly-warm audience, scrolling.
**Job of the content:** get noticed, get remembered, earn a follow/save —
nothing more.
**Depth:** shallow by design. One idea, delivered fast. No multi-step
frameworks, no case detail that needs setup.
**Length:** IG/YouTube Shorts 30-90 sec, X posts/threads, single-idea
carousels.
**CTA intensity: soft.** "Follow for more," "Save this," a comment
question. Never a direct project/enquiry ask — that's a mismatch this
early and reads as pushy to a cold viewer.
**Brief anchor:** §10's short-form hook examples ("A 1 crore home can
still fail if planning is wrong," "Never give your land for JV before
checking this") are TOFU by design — sharp, single-idea, no setup needed.

### MOFU — Consideration
**Who it's for:** people who've noticed Rengadurai (via TOFU content or
search) and are now evaluating — genuinely planning a build/investment/
interior project and sizing him up against other options.
**Job of the content:** prove expertise, build trust, teach something
real. This is where "why should a premium client trust Rengadurai" gets
answered in depth.
**Depth:** the brief's long-form standard — frameworks, multi-point
breakdowns, process education.
**Length:** 6-10 min for most, up to 20-35 min for landowner/investor deep
dives per brief §9.
**CTA intensity: medium.** Subscribe, save the playlist, a mention of the
relevant service woven in near the close — but the video's job is trust,
not the ask.
**Brief anchor:** §9's long-form YouTube questions ("What does he know that
an ordinary contractor/broker doesn't? What risk does he help the client
avoid?") are MOFU questions — they're for someone already engaged enough
to sit through 8+ minutes.

### BOFU — Conversion
**Who it's for:** people who already trust him — via MOFU content, referral,
or direct search — and are close to a decision. They need proof it's real
and a frictionless path to reach out.
**Job of the content:** real proof (named projects, specifics) + a direct,
audience-specific ask.
**Depth:** doesn't need to teach a framework — the viewer is past that.
Needs specificity: this project, this budget, this outcome, this is what
working with me looks like.
**Length:** varies — a tight project case study can be 5-8 min; a
positioning piece can be shorter.
**CTA intensity: direct.** The exact brief §12 CTAs, used at full strength:
"send your project details," "DM CONSULT," named-brand routing (Square
Mount, Urban Mark).
**Brief anchor:** Category 2 (Project Case Studies) is explicitly "Purpose:
convert proof into trust" — that's BOFU language in the brief itself.

## What this means for what gets written next

**Priority order, funnel-first:**

1. **TOFU short-form scripts** — take the brief's own §10 hook list ("A 1
   crore home can still fail if planning is wrong," etc.) and turn 3-5 of
   them into actual 30-60 sec Shorts/Reels scripts. Zero dependency on
   real project facts or founder input — fully draftable now. This is the
   fastest way to close the biggest gap.
2. **Founder Journey (Category 7) as TOFU** — once Rengadurai answers the
   interview-style questions in `07-founder-journey/README.md`, that
   content should be scripted as TOFU (relatable, short), not another
   MOFU deep-dive. Closes a category gap and the TOFU gap together.
3. **Project Case Studies (Category 2) as BOFU** — once real project facts
   are available for one of the five named projects, script it explicitly
   as a conversion asset: specific, proof-heavy, direct CTA. This is the
   highest-leverage single asset we could add.

## Before writing any new script — the stage checklist

1. **Pick the stage first.** Who is this for: cold audience (TOFU),
   engaged-but-deciding (MOFU), or ready-to-act (BOFU)?
2. **Let the stage set the length and depth** — don't default to 9-10 min
   out of habit. A TOFU script that runs long is a MOFU script wearing a
   TOFU hook.
3. **Match the CTA to the stage** — soft for TOFU, medium for MOFU, direct
   (brief §12 exact line) for BOFU. Don't hard-sell a cold audience or
   soft-pedal a warm one.
4. **Log it in `content-tracker.md`** with both its category (existing
   column) and its stage (new column, added alongside this doc).
